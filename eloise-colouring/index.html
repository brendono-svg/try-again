Tips

Keep her inside the app

Android: turn on App pinning (Settings → Security), open the app, then pin it from the recent-apps screen. iPhone: turn on Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility), open the app and triple-click the side button. She then can't leave the app until you unlock the phone.

What the app can and can't do

There are no ads, links, purchases, accounts or messages. Her pictures and any photos you choose stay on this phone. The only thing fetched from the internet is the lettering font.
